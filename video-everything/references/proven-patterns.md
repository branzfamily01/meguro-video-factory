# Proven Production Patterns and Evidence

These patterns were distilled from completed Codex video work with reviewable
artifacts. Project names below are evidence anchors, not style templates. Select
a pattern; do not copy its visual style blindly.

## 1. Social fragment detective

**Input:** Threads/X fragment or screen recording with little context.  
**Method:** download fragment → transcribe distinctive phrase → identify speaker/show → locate official full source → match frame and wording → edit from best source.  
**Use:** viral clips, unattributed interviews, cropped reposts.

**Evidence:** `elon-forbes-2031`, `nishino-generation-digest`.

## 2. Exact three-minute research digest

**Input:** TED, official explainer, research interview, or seminar.  
**Method:** timed transcript → one thesis → 4–6 complete sections → exact 180-second cut plan → original audio + edited Japanese captions → chapter graphics → final-file QA.  
**Use:** education, AI research, finance, neurodiversity.

**Evidence:** `ted-talk-digests`, `money-system-digest`, `jessica-mccabe-adhd-digest`.

## 3. Hook then rewind

**Input:** Long interview with a late, high-value answer.  
**Method:** open on the truthful answer → rewind to the context that earns it → return to consequences → end on a concise statement.  
**Use:** long interviews.

**Evidence:** `altman-bottleneck-5min`.

## 4. Dual-source gap fill

**Input:** Official edit omits part of an answer, while a social excerpt preserves it.  
**Method:** use the official source as the master → fill only the missing section from the verified excerpt → label the source change → match audio and picture behavior.  
**Use:** broadcast excerpts and reposts.

**Evidence:** `nishino-generation-digest`.

## 5. Alternate-angle reuse

**Input:** A source already used in a prior digest.  
**Method:** compare prior thesis/cut plan → select non-overlapping evidence → formulate a genuinely different claim → retain familiar design DNA without duplicating the edit.  
**Use:** content series and course libraries.

**Evidence:** `elon-forbes-2031`, `elon-forbes-ai-acceleration`, `elon-forbes-digital-medicine`, `elon-forbes-multiplanet-civilization`.

## 6. Screen-workflow tutorial

**Input:** Browser/app recording.  
**Method:** privacy scan → remove dead time → preserve reproducible cursor context → step labels and gentle focus moves → proof of result → optional BGM.  
**Use:** Codex, spreadsheets, design tools, and software demos.

**Evidence:** `codex-record-replay-hyperframes`, `chatgpt-codex-plugin-guide`, `hix-ai-slide-video`.

## 7. Faceless or privacy-safe repackage

**Input:** Screen-first or explanatory material where people or private UI should not become the subject.  
**Method:** scan the source for private UI → retain only safe ranges → reframe around the process → use meaningful diagrams/status information instead of decorative cover-ups → inspect the whole output.  
**Use:** privacy-safe demonstrations and faceless explainers.

**Evidence:** `tax2026-salaryman-recut`, `chatgpt-video-editing-wow`, `digital-succession-goldmine`.

## 8. Native-speech AI promo

**Input:** Product/course concept requiring a Japanese-speaking generated person.  
**Method:** reference image → registered identity asset → Seedance/Renoise native Japanese dialogue → confirm audio stream → separate native audio for editing → captions/CTA/BGM in HyperFrames → vertical safe-zone QA.  
**Use:** 15-second social ads and course promos.

**Evidence:** `ai-business-ad-30s`, `genai-course-promo-15s`, `seedance-jp-speaking-test-300`.

## 9. Continuous generated short film

**Input:** 30-second visual concept.  
**Method:** logline/treatment → two or more generated shots → tail-frame or reference-video continuity → inspect the real seam → retain native sound or build one soundscape → subtle HyperFrames finish.  
**Use:** brand films and cinematic experiments.

**Evidence:** `last-signal-30s`, `ai-business-ad-30s`.

## 10. Batch short-video factory

**Input:** A table of 10–30 related prompts.  
**Method:** shared format and validation schema → data-driven copy → per-row uniqueness checks → deterministic batch render → automated probe/contact sheet → per-file exception log.  
**Use:** fortune, tips, account series, and catalog content.

**Evidence:** `fortune-30-videos-hf`, six-video `money-*` series.

## 11. Music and lyric video

**Input:** Music track or song.  
**Method:** analyze beat/energy → chapter the track → place visuals and lyrics on the beat grid → maintain lyric readability → assemble sections → loudness and sync QA.  
**Use:** MVs, visualizers, kinetic lyrics.

**Evidence:** `slap-bass-jazz-groove-mv`, `slap-bass-jazz-human-lyrics-mv`.

## 12. Session-to-content bundle

**Input:** Webinar, Q&A, or seminar recording.  
**Method:** full transcript → SRT → three-minute digest → contact sheet → editable summary slides → source package.  
**Use:** archives, lead generation, internal training.

**Evidence:** `second-question-digest`.

## 13. Render rescue without creative drift

**Input:** Correct preview but failed/slow final render.  
**Method:** preserve the approved edit → normalize hard sources → use supported cache/chunk/hardware paths → verify assembled MP4 → compare contact sheet to preview.  
**Use:** long 1080p/30fps projects, AV1 sources, sparse-keyframe social clips.

**Evidence:** `nishino-generation-digest`, `pieter-levels-latest-digest`, `slap-bass-jazz-human-lyrics-mv`.
